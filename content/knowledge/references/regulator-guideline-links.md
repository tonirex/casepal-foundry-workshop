## Usage note

CasePal grounds its answers on the **synthetic corpus in this repo** — not on any live regulator site. The links above are here so *facilitators* can point curious attendees at real reference material after the workshop.

If a lab prompt asks CasePal about a topic not covered by the synthetic corpus, CasePal should say so plainly rather than fetch from a live external site. That behaviour is enforced by the Lab 3 guardrails and evaluated by the "regulatory-neutrality" custom evaluator.
